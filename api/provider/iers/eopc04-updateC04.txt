Changes in 2020 C04 / Bulletin B

--------------------------------
2026 March, 9

Robert Heinkelmann  (GFZ) noticed erroneous pole rates for the year 1984 
(private communication). The problem has been solved : C04 pole rate values have been 
recalculated by cubic splines derivative, and updated accordingly. 


--------------------------------
2026 February, 5
 
Fourth update of the 2020 C04 solution (IERS message numero 547)

Following a decision taken during the IERS DB No. 81, the pole
coordinates (xp, yp) and their rates (xrt, yrt) are updated on the
period 1/1/2021 - 31/12/2024 by taking the values corresponding to the
ITRF2020-u2024. 

<https://itrf.ign.fr/ftp/pub/itrf/itrf2020-u2024/ITRF2020-u2024_EOP-F1.DAT>

This change eliminates systematic effects coming from the IGS finals
solution, that was not fully recalculated in the ITRF2020 solution over
this period. The name of the updated series remains unchanged as
well as their path:

https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/eop/eopc04

Values for (xp, yp) and (xrt, yrt) sampled at 12h UTC are exactly those
of ITRF2020-u2024, whereas values at 0h result from a spline
interpolation.

The change is effective from February 5, 2026 (Thursday).

The former solution is archived in the repository
 
https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/eop/eopc04_20_v3

Beyond February 5, 2026 these series no longer have any future
updates.  
 

--------------------------------
2025 June, 5

Third update of the 2020 C04 solution (IERS message numero 534)

Following a decision taken during the IERS DB #80 in Vienna, Austria, the pole coordinates 
(xp, yp) and their rates (xrt, yrt) will be updated on the period 1/1/2021 - 24/2/2024 by 
taking the values corresponding to the ITRF2020-u2023. 

This change will eliminate systematic effects coming from the IGS finals solution, that was 
not fully recalculated in the ITRF2020 solution over this period. The name of the updated 
series will remain unchanged as well as their path: 

https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/eop/eopc04 

Values for (xp, yp) and (xrt, yrt) sampled at 12h UTC are exactly those of ITRF2020-u2023, 
whereas values at 0h result from a Lagrange interpolation.

The change will be effective from June 5, 2025 (Thursday). 

The former solution will be archived in the repository
 
https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/eop/eopc04_20_v2 

Beyond June 5, 2025 these series will no longer have any future updates.  


----------------
2023 December 12

On December 5 Robert Heinkelman (GFZ) reported to me outliers in UT1 values 
during the year 2017, confirmed by Manuela Seitz (TU Munchen). We fixed the origin of the 
problem and recalculated the C04 UT1 values for that period. That 1rst version of the C04 
series was put in the folder https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/eop/eopc04_20_v1

-------------
2023 April 24

From that date we produce of version of the 20 C04 celestial pole offsets 
dPsi, dEps with respect to IAU 1980 precession-nutation model and named: 
eopc04.dPsi_dEps.IAU1980.1962-now.  

-------------
2023 April 21

Until 2023 April 6, extrapolation of the biases of the inter-technique pole coordinate solution 
entering the C04 over 2019-2021 to the period 2021-2023, and removed accordingly from those series 
before combination. But, that caused spurious biases with other pole coordinate series, 
as Maria Davis points out biases, especially in x: +30 µas in the differences USNO Final / JPL - C04. 

From April 21, intra-technique solutions entering in the C04 over the period 2021-current date are 
aligned with the IGS repro3 solution recalculated in the ITRF 2020 by Paul Rebishung (IGN).

The UT1, celestial pole offsets dX, dY are aligned with ICRF 3, namely the IVS quarterly solution 
released on 2022 0ctober 31.

------------
2023 April 8

Maria Davis (USNO) noticed that the pole coordinates at 0hUTC 
were unsually noisy over the period 2021-April 2023. The problem was fixed. 


----------------
2023 February 14

The 14 C04 ERP solution is replaced by the series 20 C04, consistent 
with ITRF 2020. Repertory remains the same. The format is changed, pole rates 
coordinates are included, except for the file eoppc04_IAU2000.62-now. 

See https://hpiers.obspm.fr/iers/eop/eopc04/eopc04.txt for the description 
of the new solution. 

The 14 C04 series will be produced until a non defined date, and 
put in the neighbouring directory eopc04_14.
