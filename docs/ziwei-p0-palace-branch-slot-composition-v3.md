## Lineage and negative candidate boundaries

The exact 1871 catalog candidate remains [CiNii BD19656670](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BD19656670): `游藝録 6卷`, (清)兪樾, `同治10 [1871]`. No public 1871 page image was located in this unit, and no direct text, colophon, or byte comparison was performed. The 1871 scan/lineage target therefore remains open.

The research also reviewed and rejected or held two same-title/adjacent-work candidates:

- `CADAL01032231 遊藝錄.djvu` is a same-title collision: the Commons record identifies 李佃, 醉月山房, 清光緒二十年（1894）, not 俞樾’s `游藝錄`. It does not enter the graph.
- `NLC416-12jh004539-48693 華山陳希夷先生飛星紫微斗數原旨` is a separate Ziwei work, not `俞樾 游藝錄`; it remains outside this composition.

Two further acquisition/review leads remain outside the graph:

- The [CiNii 1968 reprint record](https://cir.nii.ac.jp/crid/1971149384876944315) identifies `中國文獻出版社, 1968.9` and notes that it is a facsimile of the `同治10年(1871)` printing, including `游藝録`. No direct bytes or page images were obtained, so it is an acquisition lead rather than a 1871 text or lineage witness.
- The [National Digital Library of Korea `紫微斗數方書` scan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CNTS-00047996572_%E7%B4%AB%E5%BE%AE%E6%96%97%E6%95%B8%E6%96%B9%E6%9B%B8.pdf) is an anonymous handwritten, separate Ziwei work. Its PDF bytes were hashed outside Git (`SHA-256 b21bbf3e2c7cdada4153f847ff9f359dbb29e71998e1f931417d108b571b23c3`); reviewed pages 1–28, including selected direct review of pages 6–16, did not surface a complete palace-name/branch/physical-slot/ordinal binding. It remains held outside the `游藝錄` graph.

These candidates are recorded with `doesNotEnterGraph: true`; neither changes source authority, semantic authority, readiness, or activation.

The text-only [Wikisource transcription of `紫微斗數全書` volume II](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/%E7%B4%AB%E5%BE%AE%E6%96%97%E6%95%B8%E5%85%A8%E6%9B%B8/%E5%8D%B7%E4%BA%8C) independently repeats the conventional palace-order wording, but it has not been promoted as a primary scan witness because the transcription does not provide a verified scan identity and physical diagram-slot context.
