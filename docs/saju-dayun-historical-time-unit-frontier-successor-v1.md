# 起運 historical time-unit frontier: `辰`·`時辰`·`時`·`刻`

## Status

- **Scope:** bounded successor for the historical time-unit frontier in 起運 arithmetic.
- **Evidence level:** direct page observation where an institutional derivative and target page are identified; public derivatives and prior audit notes remain provenance-separated.
- **Current result:** `辰` and `時` are both directly attested in the same arithmetic tradition, but no direct equality `辰 = 時辰 = 時` was established.
- **Blocked:** raw NLC machine binding, exact printed locator where not visible, textual lineage, semantic authority, modern conversion, timezone, API, rounding, readiness, and production activation.
- **Preservation:** this additive document does not alter existing audits, large source PDFs, or unrelated dirty work.

## Bounded conclusion

The inspected pages support a role distinction, not a unit identity:

| label | directly observed arithmetic context | bounded interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `辰` | `一辰十歲`; `三千六百日為一辰之十歲` | macro-scale label used in the stated 大運 relation |
| `時` | `一日之終晝夜周而有十二時`; `三十六時`; `多一時`; `欠一時`; worked-example `申時`/`己巳時` | intra-day count, residual, or time-of-day label |
| `時辰` | the exact phrase `一時辰得十日之數` was not reproduced in the current reread of NLC `06857` pp.105–106 | crosswalk to `辰` or `時` unresolved; a prior audit assertion is retained only as an unresolved locator lead |
| `刻` | `大刻/小刻`, `正一刻`, and outputs retaining `刻` in a worked example | source-local subunit or residual notation; no general conversion promoted |

Accordingly, the current frontier advances only to: **`辰` and `時` co-occur with distinct visible roles; `時辰` remains an unresolved label crosswalk.** It does not establish that a `辰` is a `時辰`, that a `時辰` is a `時`, or that any of these labels can be replaced by a modern fixed-duration unit.

## Direct page evidence

### 1. NLC 《五行精紀》, `data_892 / 411999013122 / 114503.0`

The official record and reader identity are:

- [NLC record](http://read.nlc.cn/allSearch/searchDetail?searchType=24&showType=1&indexName=data_892&fid=411999013122)
- [NLC reader object](http://read.nlc.cn/OutOpenBook/OpenObjectBook?aid=892&bid=114503.0)
- [Public derivative record](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:NLC892-411999013122-114503_%E4%BA%94%E8%A1%8C%E7%B2%BE%E7%B4%80_%E7%AC%AC4%E5%86%8A.pdf)

For this bounded check, the public derivative was downloaded outside the repository and rendered at PDF pages 105–106. It is not treated as the NLC raw byte stream or as a machine-level binding to the institutional object.

- derivative PDF SHA-256: `6519fbdc0fa25272bf6aae0fdac8c73107c0f6b852a1b0beebc655344ec2812d`
- rendered page 105 SHA-256: `75b1154bddf4c603ec29b1cb1f787707c372017cd8b364f310dea4ca87a38a3e`
- rendered page 106 SHA-256: `e8c02971fa0f8762c72956a27d29af0f3f44fc6d5ed0112f74371b16576838c9`

The directly legible text includes:

```text
五行精紀 卷第三十三
大運
運行則一辰十歲折除乃三日為年
一日之終晝夜周而有十二時
三十六時乃見三百六十日為一歲之數
一月之中有三百六十時
折除節氣算計三千六百日為一辰之十歲
節氣日時為數
```

The same page range contains worked-example labels such as `己巳時` and the day/time endpoint context. Thus `辰` and `時` are both present in the calculation, but the observed wording does not say that they are the same unit.

The exact string `一時辰得十日之數` was **not** visible in this fresh reread of PDF pp.105–106. Existing audit material records that phrase as a prior derivative observation, but this successor does not re-promote it without an exact page locator. It remains a locator-recovery item, not a direct result of this check.

### 2. NLC 《三命通會》, direct derivative pp.114–115

The inspected pages directly show:

```text
古人以大運則一辰十歲折除以三日為年
一月之終晦朔周而有三十日。一日之終晝夜周而有十二時。
三十六時乃見三百六十日為一歲之數
五日三時
六十三時
六百三十日
若春在寅時則多一時乃零一旬也。若欠一時乃借一旬。
```

This is direct evidence that `時` participates in residual 起運 arithmetic. It does not supply a direct `時辰` equality or prove a copy-level relationship with the NLC 《五行精紀》 item.

### 3. NCL 《新命理探原》, p.82

The official [NCL record](https://taiwanebook.ncl.edu.tw/en/book/NCL-000002203) derivative directly states:

```text
必足三十六時方算一日。
必足三日方算一歲。
若餘一時則為多十天。
若少一時則為欠十天。
```

This independently corroborates a source-local `時` residual rule. It does not establish that `時` is identical with `辰` or `時辰`, and its `刻`-bearing worked example does not by itself provide a general `刻` conversion rule.

### 4. 《珞琭子》 arithmetic witnesses

The inspected NLC 《珞琭子賦注》 page directly shows the literal sequence `凡三日有三十六時`, `三百六十日為一歲`, and `一辰之十歲`. The CADAL 《珞琭子》 derivative pages likewise show the sequence `一辰十歲`, `一日之內十二時`, `凡三日有三十六時`, `三百六十日為一歲`, and `三千六百日為一辰之十歲`, with `一日主四箇月` appearing in the 李仝 gloss layer in the previously audited page range.

These repeated textual relations are bounded corroboration across material witnesses. They do not establish direct textual lineage, a common exemplar, or semantic authority. The literal character `辰` is retained; it is not normalized to `時` or `時辰`.

### 5. `刻` as a source-local finer notation

The inspected 《命理集成》 page uses `一日之終晝夜周而有十二時` together with `大刻/小刻` terminology. The exact sub-刻 count is not promoted because the page’s variant-sensitive small-unit wording was not sufficiently stable for a normalized rule. A Harvard 《三才發秘》 page preserves inputs such as `巳初二刻` and `酉正二刻` and outputs retaining `刻`, including `三日零九時二刻` and `二十五日四時六刻`. This shows that at least one source-local worked example retains `刻` rather than automatically erasing it. It does not establish a universal truncation, rounding, or modern minute conversion.

## Crosswalk decision

| proposition | result | reason |
|---|---|---|
| `辰` is used in the macro 大運 relation | **direct** | repeated literal `一辰十歲` and `一辰之十歲` |
| `時` is used for intra-day and residual arithmetic | **direct** | `十二時`, `三十六時`, `六十三時`, `多一時/欠一時` and worked time labels |
| `辰` and `時` occur in one calculation tradition | **direct, bounded** | same pages/nearby arithmetic contain both labels |
| `辰 = 時辰` | **unresolved** | no equality statement or exact crosswalk in the freshly inspected target pages |
| `時辰 = 時` | **unresolved** | exact `時辰` phrase was not reproduced at the target locator |
| `一辰 = 36時` or another fixed modern duration | **not promoted** | textual arithmetic relations were not converted into a modern physical-time equivalence |
| `刻` has a universal conversion to `時` | **unresolved** | only source-local `大刻/小刻` and worked residual notation were directly observed |

## Historical and production boundary

This successor records direct wording and local calculation context only. It does not promote:

- modern two-hour `時辰`, fifteen-minute `刻`, or any other modern time conversion;
- timezone, astronomical API, endpoint inclusion/exclusion, rounding, or numerical implementation rules;
- a common ancestor, edition order, textual independence, or specific transmission lineage;
