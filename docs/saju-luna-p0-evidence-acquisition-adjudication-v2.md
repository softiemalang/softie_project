## Unit B text boundary

The derivative pages 105–106 provide one contiguous visual passage. The six operational fragments are stored in the machine-readable artifact as `verbatimFragments`, with separate arithmetic restatements:

1. `運行則一辰十歲` → one 辰 is ten years.
2. `折除乃三日為年` → three days are one year.
3. `凡三日有三十六時` → three days are thirty-six hours.
4. `乃見三百六十日，為一歲之數` → thirty-six hours are 360 days / one year.
5. `一日十二時，得一百二十日，為四箇月之數` → twelve hours are 120 days / four months.
6. `一時辰得十日之數` → one hour is ten days.

The worked example is retained as bounded derivative-page wording. `六十三時 = 五日三時` and `六百三十日 = 一歲奇九月` are arithmetic restatements, not implementation authority. The later approximate-method reading `一歲奇八月` is kept separate from the candidate’s normalized wording.
