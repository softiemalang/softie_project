# Ziwei P0 palace branch-slot composition v9

v9 is an additive successor to v8. It records a newly reviewed Zhejiang Library physical scan of `春在堂全書三十六種 第3冊` as a direct named-palace corroboration. The scan is catalog-described as a `清末石印本` with upper and lower twenty-line sections, white mouth, and four-sided single border. It is therefore a distinct physical and lithographic-format witness from the already recorded Zhejiang Library `第25冊` scan, which is catalog-described as a late compiled/reprinted ten-line edition.

The new scan is not treated as a 1871 page witness, proof of block identity, independent semantic authority, or complete four-field map. No predecessor artifact, protected asset, production rule, readiness state, remote database, deployment, commit, or push is changed.
